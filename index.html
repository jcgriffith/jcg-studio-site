About

I’m JC.

A designer working across physical and digital products, brands and services. I work with ambitious teams launching something new, and pursue ideas of my own.

I want to make life more exciting, meaningful and full of possibility. Tools that help people learn, create and prosper. Experiences that bring us together, spark curiosity and make life more fun. Products that expand what we can do while helping us care for ourselves and the world.

I work across product and brand to carry an idea into real life: what it is, how it works, how people discover it, and how it becomes part of their lives. The product gives the idea form and function. The brand helps people understand it, care about it, and find a place for it.

JCG Studio brings years of design craft together with today’s AI workflows. You work directly with me, from early questions through the finished details. AI expands what I can explore and produce; I make the design decisions and carry them through.
