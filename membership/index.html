FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? hello@jcg.studio

Pick a question. Every answer opens three more.

Q.01How does the queue work?

Add as many requests as you want. You set the order; I work through them one at a time on Standard, two at a time on Pro. When one ships, the next one starts.

Q.02How fast will I get work back?

Something usable lands about every two business days: a page, a deck section, a social set, a component. Larger work is split into chunks and delivered in sequence, so you're reviewing progress every couple of days instead of waiting weeks.

Q.03Do we have meetings?

Very few. We start with a short kickoff call to align on goals, audience, and brand. After that, everything runs async through the queue. Pro adds a Slack channel if you want one.

Q.04What about larger projects, like a full design system or site?

That's what the Membership is built for. A design system or a full site becomes a sequence of requests: tokens, then components, then templates, then pages. Each chunk is usable on its own, and you can reprioritize between them.

Q.05What if I only have one project?

Subscribe for a month, get it done, then pause or cancel. Your files are yours either way.

Q.06How does pausing work?

Pause anytime. Billing pauses with it, and when you're ready to resume, your queue and assets are exactly where you left them.

Q.07What's included, and what isn't?

Included: design systems, decks, social, email, investor and sales collateral, marketing sites and landing pages, and adjacent design work that helps you ship.

Not included: product engineering (app backends, auth, data), video production, large illustration or 3D projects, print production, and ad spend. If you're not sure, ask before you subscribe.

Q.08What's an "AI marketing site"?

A marketing site I design and build myself, with AI in the loop, instead of handing a mockup to a separate dev team. It ships faster, and because it's built on your design system, your team (and your AI tools) can keep adding pages without it drifting off-brand.

Q.09What does "AI-readable design system" mean?

Your brand, written down as tokens and plain-language rules, not just a Figma file. Tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor can read it, so the UI and copy they generate follow your system instead of guessing.

Q.10Who does the work?

I do, personally. No handoffs to juniors, no offshore team. You work directly with the person designing and building.

Q.11Are there refunds?

Refunds aren't offered for completed billing cycles. You can pause or cancel anytime.
